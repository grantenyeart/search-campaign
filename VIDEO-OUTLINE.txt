================================================================
SEARCH CAMPAIGNS: THE PRODUCT TRAINING
Video outline and what to say
Target length 12 to 15 minutes
================================================================

WHO THIS IS FOR
Your closers and setters. Not clients. They need two things out
of this video: how the product actually works, and the words to
explain it on a call without sounding like they memorised a page.

THE ONE THING THIS VIDEO HAS TO DO
Answer "why would I have you run it instead of running it myself"
so completely that it stops being an objection. Everything else
is supporting material.

The answer is the auction. A business owner assumes Google is an
auction where the biggest wallet wins, so paying you looks like
paying a middleman. Once they understand the highest bid does not
win, the whole conversation changes. Lead with that.

LANGUAGE RULES, SAY THEM ON CAMERA
  Internally: PPC is fine.
  To a client: "Google Search Campaign." Never "PPC."
  Never say "LSA." Say "your Google pay per lead ads."
  Never say "that has quite literally never happened." It has.
  The guarantee is spoken only. Never a slide, never in the pitch.


----------------------------------------------------------------
1  COLD OPEN                                        about 45 sec
----------------------------------------------------------------
Do not start with a definition. Start with the objection.

SAY SOMETHING LIKE:
  "Every owner you pitch this to is going to think the same
   thing. Google Ads is just an auction. Whoever pays the most
   shows up first. So why would I pay you on top of paying
   Google?

   By the end of this you are going to be able to take that
   apart in about ninety seconds, because the premise is wrong.
   The highest bid does not win. It has never been how it works."

Then tell them what the video covers in one breath.


----------------------------------------------------------------
2  WHAT A SEARCH CAMPAIGN IS                          1 to 2 min
----------------------------------------------------------------
Keep it short. They have the deck for detail.

Cover:
  - Somebody types a buying search. "Mobile detailing near me."
  - Our ad is the text ad right under the pay per lead ads.
  - They click, they land on a page we built for that search,
    not a homepage.
  - They book, call or fill the form. That is a conversion.
  - Client pays Google per click, directly, on their own card.

SAY:
  "Pay per lead ads get them on the map. The Search Campaign
   gets them past the cap. They stack, they do not compete."

Do not go deeper here. The next section is the video.


----------------------------------------------------------------
3  THE AUCTION. THE CENTREPIECE                       4 to 5 min
----------------------------------------------------------------
Slow down. This is the part they will actually use on calls.

3a  The formula
    Ad Rank = your bid  x  your Quality Score  +  your ad extras

    Say it plainly: there are three levers and money is only one
    of them.

3b  Quality Score, 1 to 10, on every keyword
    Google scores every single keyword out of ten, live, every
    time somebody searches. Three things go into it:

      1. Expected click rate. Do people actually click this ad.
      2. Ad relevance. Does the ad answer what they typed.
      3. Landing page experience. What the page does once they
         arrive. Speed, mobile, does it match the promise.

    SAY:
      "Every keyword in the account gets a report card. Out of
       ten. Updated every time somebody searches. And two of
       those three grades have nothing to do with money."

3c  THE WORKED EXAMPLE. Put this on screen.
    Two detailers, same town, same search.

      Guy running it himself       We run it
      bids $8                      bids $5
      Quality Score 4              Quality Score 9
      8 x 4 = Ad Rank 32           5 x 9 = Ad Rank 45

    Our client wins the higher spot while bidding three dollars
    less per click.

    SAY:
      "He is paying more and losing. Not because we outspent
       him. Because we out built him."

3d  It gets better, because the score also sets the price
    What you actually pay per click is based on the competitor
    below you divided by your own Quality Score. Your score is
    the denominator. Higher score, lower price.

    Score 6 to 10, Google discounts your clicks.
    Score 4 or under, you pay a premium for the same position.

    SAY:
      "So the score does two jobs. It decides where you show up,
       and it decides what you pay to be there. A bad score
       means you pay more for a worse spot. That is the trap
       every owner running it themselves falls into."

3e  The line to close the section
    "You cannot buy your way to the top of Google. You can only
     earn your way there. Earning it is the part we do."

ONE HONESTY NOTE, SAY IT ON CAMERA
    Bid times Quality Score is the simplified version. The real
    auction also factors device, location, time of day and the
    ad assets. Tell your reps that, so if a sharp prospect
    pushes, they say "that is the simple version, there are a
    few more inputs" instead of getting caught out.


----------------------------------------------------------------
4  WHY THEY CANNOT DO THIS ALONE                      2 to 3 min
----------------------------------------------------------------
Now the six ways they burn money land differently, because every
one of them is a Quality Score problem.

  1. No negative keywords. They pay for "how to detail a car
     yourself" all day. Kills click rate, kills the score.
  2. They send clicks to the homepage. Kills landing page
     experience, which is a third of the grade.
  3. They bid on everything at once. Budget spread thin, no
     keyword ever gets enough data to score well.
  4. No tracking. They cannot tell which half worked.
  5. Set it and forget it. Google keeps spending either way.
  6. They quit at week three, right when it stops learning and
     starts producing.

SAY:
  "Notice what three of those six are. They are not budget
   problems. They are build problems. He could double his
   budget tomorrow and every one of those is still true."


----------------------------------------------------------------
5  WHAT WE ACTUALLY BUILD                             1 to 2 min
----------------------------------------------------------------
Tie each piece to the grade it moves. That is what makes this
sound like a service instead of a fee.

  Keyword research and negatives     -> click rate
  Ad copy written per ad group       -> ad relevance
  Landing page built for the search  -> landing page experience
  Conversion tracking                -> proves it worked
  Ongoing management                 -> the score is live, so
                                        the work has to be too

SAY:
  "Every single thing we do maps to one of the three grades.
   That is the whole service. We are not managing a budget. We
   are raising a score."


----------------------------------------------------------------
6  THE MONEY                                          1 to 2 min
----------------------------------------------------------------
Use the deck for the ladder. On camera just cover:
  - $50 a day is the number that works, and what that produced.
  - It is their card and their Google account. We never mark up
    the ad spend.
  - Month to month.

Point them at the deck for the price ladder rather than reading
numbers out, so the video does not go stale when pricing moves.


----------------------------------------------------------------
7  PUTTING IT ON A CALL                               1 to 2 min
----------------------------------------------------------------
Give them the ninety second version to actually say out loud.

  "Most people think Google is just an auction, highest bidder
   wins. It is not. Google scores every keyword out of ten on
   three things: whether people click your ad, whether the ad
   matches the search, and what your page does when they land.
   Your position is your bid times that score. So somebody with
   a great score and a smaller bid beats somebody with a big
   bid and a bad one, and pays less per click doing it. Running
   it yourself, you are almost always the second guy. We do the
   work that moves the score."

Then shut up and let them respond.

CLOSE THE VIDEO ON:
  "You are not selling ad management. You are selling the three
   grades. Anybody can place a bid. Almost nobody can move the
   score."


----------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU HIT RECORD
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Have the worked example on screen. It is the one thing people
  will screenshot and remember.

  Keep the pricing thin. Point at the deck instead, so you do
  not have to re-record when the ladder changes.

  Say the language rules out loud, early. Search Campaign, not
  PPC. Pay per lead ads, not LSA. Reps repeat what they hear.

  Do not mention the guarantee. Not once. It has its own slide
  in the module and it is spoken only.
