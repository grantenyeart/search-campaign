What the video covers
  1. What a Search Campaign is. Someone searches the service, our ad shows up.
  2. The highest bid does not win. Google judges three things: the bid, the Quality Score and the ad extras.
  3. Why owners can't run it alone. They blow the budget on keywords that never convert.

The one thing to walk away with. Every owner thinks Google is an auction where the biggest wallet wins, so paying us looks like paying a middleman. It isn't. You can't buy your way to the top of Google. You earn it, and earning it is the part we do.
